Help Center

How can we help?

Short answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? The contact page is one click away.

What is Syllabus+?

Syllabus+ is the learning branch of noname. Pick your subject and year (S1–S7), then read interactive articles with rendered maths, demos and quizzes.

How do I change the language?

Use the globe menu in the top bar to switch between English, German and French. Your choice carries across pages.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is free to browse. Signing in (top right) is optional and is used to remember preferences as the ecosystem grows.

A subject says “Coming soon”. Why?

We add material gradually. Greyed-out subjects exist but don’t have content yet — check back, or ask for a specific topic on the contact page.

Something looks broken.

Try a refresh first. If it persists, tell us on the feedback page with the page name and your browser — it really helps.
