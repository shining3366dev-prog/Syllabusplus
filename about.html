About

One home. Many tools.

noname is a small, growing ecosystem of focused web tools. We build one thing at a time, and we build it to last.

What noname is

noname is an umbrella for a handful of carefully made web apps. Instead of one bloated site that tries to do everything, each tool is its own “branch” — small, fast and focused — sharing the same calm design, the same three languages, and the same front door. Open the toolbox (the squares in the top-right) to move between them.

Branches

A branch is a self-contained app under the noname umbrella. It has its own logo and its own home, but it inherits the ecosystem’s chrome, accounts and design for free. New branches should feel like they always belonged. Today there is one branch live — Syllabus+ — with more in the workshop.

Vision

A calm, coherent place where small tools compound into something bigger than the sum of its parts. The opposite of feature bloat: every screen earns its place, the look stays quiet and timeless, and the whole thing should feel like it respects your time and your attention.

  • Less, but better. If it isn’t used, it’s cut.
  • Quiet by default. Warm paper, ink text, generous space — content first.
  • One ecosystem. Shared chrome, shared accounts, independent apps.

Roadmap

Where things are headed — rough, honest, and subject to change.

  • Now — Syllabus+ (S1–S7 study materials, articles, quizzes) in English, German and French.
  • Next — accounts that remember your language, progress and saved items across branches.
  • Later — more branches in the toolbox, and deeper links between them.
